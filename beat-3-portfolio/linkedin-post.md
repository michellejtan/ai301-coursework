This fall I added a missing constant to CTSM, an open source climate model, so the number of months in a year is defined once instead of typed out in three places. My pull request is open and awaiting review, after I found that most of the five-year-old request had already been done and narrowed my work to the missing piece.

Three habits shaped the work:

✅ Verify first. I read the project's history before building, and asked the maintainer what was still wanted instead of guessing.
✅ Prove it. I wrote tests that failed before my change and passed after, and the project's full set of 680 tests passed.
✅ Own the AI. I reviewed every line AI helped me write, disclosed that help, and submitted the work myself, as the project's rules ask.

Thank you to the maintainers for the warm welcome and clear guidance.
